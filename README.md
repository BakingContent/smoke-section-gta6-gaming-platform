# Smoke-Section-GTA6-Gaming-Platform

Designing a full-stack gaming analytics platform targeting GTA players while building and mastering AWS cloud infrastructure and Python. Covers cloud architecture, data pipelines, and backend API development — designed for the GTA 6 community launch.

🚀 What is Smoke Section?

Smoke Section is a full gaming platform and community brand built for the GTA 6 era. We're more than an analytics tool — we're a destination for players, content creators, and communities to connect, compete, and grow together.
When GTA 6 launches, millions of players will flood the market with no native tools for tracking performance, finding squads, or building community. Smoke Section fills that gap — across a web platform, YouTube, Twitch, and Discord.
Whether you're a casual player trying to improve, a competitive grinder tracking your stats, or a content creator looking to grow your audience — Smoke Section is your home base.
